A quick note on security

Your data is handled with care.

Because this app syncs with your email to track bank receipts, it needs inbox access. Here's a transparent breakdown of exactly how that works and what protects you.

01 · Passwords
I do NOT want your real email password.

You will never type your actual email password into this app. Instead, you generate a one-time App Password directly through Google or Yahoo. It grants sync permission only — and you can revoke it instantly at any time without changing your real password.

02 · Inbox Privacy
The app does not read your personal emails.

The app is strictly coded to only look for emails from specific bank sender addresses (e.g. UOB, DBS). Everything else is filtered out at the server level. It reads the dollar amount and date, logs the transaction, and ignores the rest of your inbox.

03 · Data Security
Your data is locked in a secure vault.

The app runs on enterprise platforms used by major tech companies. Vercel encrypts all data in transit, and Supabase enforces Row Level Security — mathematically guaranteeing that only you can view your own expenses.
